# Voice clip licensing

The GPL-3.0 licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers this repository's code. It does **not** cover the
speech clips under `src/FactionsUI/Content/Atc/`. Those were synthesised with
[Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) voice models, each trained on a third-party dataset, and each
voice's clips are subject to that voice's upstream terms below. This project grants no rights in them
beyond what those terms allow.

Every clip was processed after synthesis: resampled from 22.05 kHz to 24 kHz, band-limited to
300 Hz–3.4 kHz with light drive as a radio voice, and stored as 8-bit μ-law. The procedure is in
`scripts/atc-audio/`.

| Clips | Voice model | Dataset | Upstream terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `names/`, `phrase/`, `spelled/` (speaker 0) | `en_GB-alan-medium` | Alan Pope's voice, Mycroft AI Mimic 3 (`apope_low`), fine-tuned from `en_US-lessac` | Mycroft AI: "All Rights Reserved"; plus the Lessac terms below |
| `speaker/1/` | `en_GB-northern_english_male-medium` | OpenSLR SLR83, English accents of the British Isles, © 2018, 2019 Google, Inc.; fine-tuned from `en_US-lessac` | CC BY-SA 4.0; plus the Lessac terms below |
| `speaker/2/` | `en_US-lessac-medium` | Blizzard Challenge 2013 Lessac data, CSTR, University of Edinburgh | Blizzard 2013 Lessac licence (research purposes) |
| `speaker/3/` | `en_US-ryan-high` | RyanSpeech (Zandie et al., 2021) | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 |

`fx/` (key-up click, chirp, static, squelch tail, alert) contains no speech. It is generated by
`scripts/atc-audio/` and is covered by the GPL-3.0 with the code.
