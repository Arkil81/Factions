# Players' guide

This guide is for players on a Space Engineers server that runs Factions. It explains what is
different from the game you know, and how to get going.

Start with **[Getting started](getting-started.md)**. It covers what Factions is, what happens when
you first arrive, and what to do in your first hour.

After that, read whichever topic you need:

- [NPC factions and how they treat you](npc-factions.md): why NPC ships hail you, follow you or
  attack you, and how reputation works.
- [Finding your way](finding-your-way.md): the scanner, the map, and how other factions detect you.
- [Trading](trading.md): station stores, services, the market window, and contracts.
- [Owning stations](owning-stations.md): getting your faction its first station, holding it, and
  building it up.
- [Diplomacy](diplomacy.md): war, peace, embargoes and pacts with the NPC factions.
- [Fleets and combat](fleets-and-combat.md): your faction's own ships, campaigns, and attacking or
  taking another faction's station.
- [Factions UI reference](factions-ui.md): the client plugin's windows, keys and settings.
- [Chat commands](chat-commands.md): every command available to players, for those without the
  client plugin or who prefer typing.

Most of this guide assumes you have the **Factions UI** client plugin installed, because much of
Factions is only visible through it. Installing it takes a few minutes:
[Installing Factions UI](../install-client.md). Where something can also be done by chat command,
the guide says so.

Costs, ranges and timings given in this guide are the defaults that Factions ships with. A server
can change them, so your server's values may differ.
